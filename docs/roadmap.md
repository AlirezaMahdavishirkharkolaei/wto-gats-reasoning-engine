# Research roadmap

The engine is the software form of the assessment framework in the author's doctoral research proposal on EU Data Act obligations under the GATS. Each planned extension corresponds to a research question, so the tool grows with the thesis and stays usable by other researchers, students and institutions afterwards.

| Extension | What it adds | Research question | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Assessment engine | Step-by-step GATS analysis with cited authorities and information gaps | Scope, applicable discipline, justification | Available |
| Data Act scenario library | Arts. 3-5, 5(3), 32, 35 and the proposed Art. 32(3a) | Scope, applicable discipline, localisation risk | Available (v2.1) |
| Versioned measure tracker | The text of each modelled provision in the adopted act and in Commission, Council and Parliament versions of the Digital Omnibus, so an analysis can be re-run on a new text | Localisation risk; effect of amendments | Planned |
| Transparency dataset | A structured, cited record of how EU digital measures are raised in WTO bodies (Council for Trade in Services, trade concerns, notifications) | Coherence and conflict prevention | Planned |
| Agreement on Electronic Commerce module | The Agreement's exceptions (GATS Article XIV mutatis mutandis, personal data protection exception) as a second legal framework once it applies between participants | Justification; coherence | Planned |
| Further validation | More adjudicated disputes reproduced as test cases (for example *Argentina - Financial Services*, *China - Publications and Audiovisual Products*) | Reliability of the method | Planned |

## Principles for every extension

* **Doctrine first.** The tool structures the analysis; it never replaces the doctrinal argument or decides holistic tests such as likeness, necessity or the chapeau.
* **Cited and dated.** Every legal text, version and record links to its official source and states the date it was checked.
* **Open.** Code under the MIT licence; datasets to be released under CC BY 4.0 with a citable DOI for each release.
